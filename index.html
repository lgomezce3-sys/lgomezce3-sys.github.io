About

I am an economist with research interests spanning international macroeconomics, financial frictions, development economics, and public policy evaluation. My work combines quantitative methods with policy-oriented questions, from exchange-rate dynamics and SME access to finance to food-price shocks, household welfare, and policy evaluation.

I hold an MSc in Econometrics and Mathematical Economics from Tilburg University and degrees in Economics and Political Science from Universidad de los Andes.
